Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Reghson Studio (“the app”, “we”), a personal video scheduling tool operated by Richard Yaw Sewa in Ghana, accesses, uses, stores and deletes information when it connects to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. Contact: reghson1@gmail.com.

1. Who uses the app

Reghson Studio is a single-user tool. It is used only by its owner to schedule and publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public and is not directed at children.

2. Information we access

When the owner connects a platform, the platform asks for permission and the app receives only what is listed below.

PlatformPermissions (scopes)Data accessed and why
TikTokuser.info.basic, video.publishOpen ID, display name and avatar, to show which account is connected; permission to upload and publish videos the owner selected.
Instagraminstagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, business_managementInstagram account ID and username, and the linked Facebook Page, to find the account; permission to publish Reels.
Facebook Pagepages_show_list, pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement, publish_videoList of Pages the owner manages, the Page name and ID, and a Page access token, to publish videos and Reels to the chosen Page.
YouTubeyoutube.upload, youtube.readonlyChannel name and ID, to show which channel is connected; permission to upload videos.

The app also stores the content the owner creates in it: video files, captions, hashtags, chosen times and the result of each publish attempt (for example the post ID or an error message).

The app does not access or collect followers' or viewers' personal data, direct messages, contacts, or payment information, and it does not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

3. How we use information

Information is used only to: show which accounts are connected, publish the owner's scheduled videos at the chosen time, refresh access when it expires, and show whether each post succeeded. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training any AI model.

4. How tokens and data are stored

  • Access and refresh tokens issued by each platform are stored in the app's private database on a server controlled by the owner. They are never shown in the browser, written to public files, or sent anywhere other than back to the platform that issued them.
  • App credentials (client secrets) are kept in server configuration, not in the website or source code.
  • The app dashboard is protected by a password and connections to the app use HTTPS.
  • Uploaded videos are stored on the same server only for as long as needed to publish them, and the owner can delete them at any time.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform the owner chose to publish to (TikTok, Meta or Google), as needed to publish the content. We may disclose information only if required by law.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Revoke access in the app: open Channels and tap Disconnect next to any platform. This immediately deletes that platform's stored tokens and account details from the app's database.
  • Revoke access on the platform: TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; Facebook/Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites (or Business integrations); Google/YouTube: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Request deletion by email: email reghson1@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion”. We will delete all stored tokens, account details and uploaded content within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Videos already published stay on the platform they were posted to and can be removed there.

Facebook data deletion instructions: to delete data received from Facebook or Instagram, disconnect the channel in the app or email reghson1@gmail.com as described above.

7. YouTube API Services and Google

Reghson Studio uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. By using the YouTube features of this app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

You can revoke Reghson Studio's access to your Google data at any time through the Google security settings page. Reghson Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. TikTok and Meta

Use of TikTok features is also subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Privacy Policy. Use of Facebook and Instagram features is subject to the Meta Terms, the Meta Privacy Policy and the Meta Platform Terms. Reghson Studio is not endorsed by or affiliated with TikTok, Meta or Google.

9. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change and the new version will be posted on this page.
