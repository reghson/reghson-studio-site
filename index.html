Reghson Studio

A personal content scheduler that lets the creator @reghson.star plan and publish their own short videos to TikTok, Instagram, a Facebook Page and YouTube from one place.

What the app does

Reghson Studio is a private, single-user scheduling tool built and used by Richard Yaw Sewa (Ghana), the creator behind the Red & Green frog story videos on TikTok (@reghson.star), Facebook, Instagram and YouTube.

The owner uploads a finished video, writes a caption and hashtags, picks the channels and a time (in Ghana time, GMT), and the app publishes the video to the owner's own accounts at that time. It is not offered to the public, does not post on behalf of other people, and does not collect data about viewers.

  • Weekly content calendar shown in Africa/Accra time
  • Video upload with caption, hashtags and privacy settings
  • Quick time slots for UK and US evening audiences
  • Status tracking for each post (scheduled, posted, failed)
  • Connect or disconnect each platform at any time

Platforms and permissions

♪

TikTok

Uses Login Kit and the Content Posting API (user.info.basic, video.publish) to show the connected account name and publish videos the owner has chosen.

◎

Instagram

Uses the Instagram Graph API (instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish) to publish Reels to the owner's professional account.

f

Facebook Page

Uses the Pages API (pages_show_list, pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement, publish_video) to post videos and Reels to the owner's Page.

▶

YouTube

Uses the YouTube Data API (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly) to upload videos and Shorts to the owner's channel and read its name.

How it works: the owner signs in to each platform through that platform's official login page and grants permission. Reghson Studio never sees the owner's passwords. Access can be removed at any time, in the app or in each platform's settings. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
